# Executive Summary  
**EduPro Learner Demographics & Course Enrollment Behavior Analysis**

**Prepared for:** Toronto Government Parks, Forestry & Recreation  
**Date:** March 2026  
**Analysis Period:** 2025 Academic Year  

---

## Purpose

This analysis provides foundational learner intelligence for the EduPro online learning platform. It answers who the learners are and how they choose courses, enabling data-driven decisions in course design, outreach, and inclusive growth.

---

## Dataset at a Glance

| Metric                  | Value     |
|-------------------------|-----------|
| Registered Learners     | 3,000     |
| Enrollment Transactions | 10,000    |
| Courses Offered         | 60        |
| Subject Categories      | 12        |
| Average Courses / Learner | 3.33    |

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Age Profile
- **26–35 age band** is the dominant cohort (1,446 learners; **48% of all enrollments**).
- **18–25** forms the second-largest group (1,121 learners; 37% of enrollments).
- **Under-18** learners (433) remain an important and active segment (15% of enrollments).

### 2. Gender Participation
- Near parity achieved: **Female 50.8%** / **Male 49.2%** of enrollments.
- Indicates healthy inclusivity across the platform.

### 3. Course Preferences
- **Beginner** courses lead at 35.7%, closely followed by **Advanced** (34.8%). Intermediate sits at 29.5%.
- Top demand categories: **Data Science**, **Finance**, **Web Development**, **Business**, and **Artificial Intelligence**.
- Free courses account for **64%** of all enrollments.

### 4. Behavioral Patterns
- Preference for Beginner and Advanced levels is consistent across all age bands and both genders.
- No strong gender-based divergence in level or category choice was observed.
- Learners take an average of 3.3 courses, suggesting opportunity for structured learning pathways.

---

## Strategic Recommendations

1. **Course Design**  
   Strengthen Intermediate-level pathways to reduce the observed dip and create clearer progression routes from Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced.

2. **Targeted Outreach**  
   - Prioritize content and marketing for the high-value **26–35** segment.  
   - Develop age-appropriate introductory pathways for the under-18 cohort.

3. **Inclusivity**  
   Maintain the current gender balance while monitoring any emerging disparities in high-demand technical categories.

4. **Engagement Strategy**  
   Leverage the 3.3 average courses-per-learner metric to design curated multi-course learning paths and sequential recommendations.

5. **Content Investment**  
   Continue expanding high-demand areas (Data Science, AI, Finance, Web Development) while monitoring under-performing categories for refresh or retirement.

---

## Conclusion

EduPro already serves a demographically balanced and engaged learner base. By translating these descriptive insights into course planning and outreach actions, the platform can move from intuition-driven to fully data-informed education delivery.
